## Description（表单里的简介）

How many cloud services are you paying for? Each one has its own dashboard and its own bill. TollCat reads what you've spent this month across 80+ cloud and AI services — Cloudflare, OpenAI, Anthropic, Vercel, and more — and puts the total in one number, on your phone, with a widget so you don't even open the app. API keys stay in the device Keychain and the phone talks directly to each vendor's official API; there's no server in between, so nobody can see your bills. Including us. Free, MIT licensed, source on GitHub.

## Maker comment（上线后第一时间贴）

Hi PH! I built TollCat because I kept getting surprised by my own cloud bills — not by any single one, but by the sum. Logging into eight dashboards to add up a number felt like something a phone should do for me.

A few things that might matter to you:

- **Your keys never leave the phone.** They're stored in the device Keychain (not iCloud, not backups), and the app talks directly to each vendor's billing API. There's no TollCat server in the middle to breach.
- **Every service comes with a setup guide** for creating a read-only API key, so you're never pasting in a key that could touch your infrastructure.
- **No billing API? There's a reading inbox.** Forward the invoice email and the number comes in anyway.
- **It's free and open source** (MIT). If it ever saves you from a scary bill, there's a tip jar in Settings — it buys the kitty a tin.

The numbers in the screenshots are demo data, not live bills. Happy to answer anything — especially "does it support X", because the supported list only grows when people ask.
